Font Language:
With a modern, approachable feel, CAPY should target clean, minimal, and modern.
The logo should be clear and concisely defined. Avoid Serif fonts and heavily bolded typefaces. 

Potential Adobe Typefaces:
- Neue Haas Grotesk Display Pro
	- too defaulty
- ITC Avant Garde Gothic Pro
	- also a little default
- Futura PT
	- little default, lines too sharp
- Novecento Sans
- Oscine
- FinalSix
	- bold and unique
- Bebas Neue SemiRounded
- Arboria Book
- Basic Sans ExtraLight It
- Darkmode DarkmodeOn
- Darkmode DarkmodeOff
- Loos Wide
- Skolar Sans Latin Condensed
- Commuters Sans
- Basic Gothic Pro
- Tablet Gothic
- Objektiv Mk3
	- little default, lines too sharp

Shape Language:
- avoid circles
	- too serious / formal
		- CAPY should feel professional but fun
- thinner line weights
- utilize lots of negative space
	- minimalistic effect
